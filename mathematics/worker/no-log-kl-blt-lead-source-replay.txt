Adversarial checks
------------------

1. Parameter/value confusion:
   Lemma 3.2 alone is insufficient. Theorem 1.2 proof first selects one
   witness per distinct t and then performs the deletion. Passed after
   preserving that step.

2. Point/field confusion:
   Two conjugate geometric points generate the same quadratic field, so the
   literal pointwise-distinct wording is too strong. Counting distinct t, or
   dividing point count by at most two, repairs the constant without changing
   the exponent. This correction is recorded explicitly.

3. Repeated fields:
   Signed squarefree t1,t2 give the same quadratic field iff t1/t2 is a
   rational square; unique signed squarefree representatives imply t1=t2.

4. Thin-set parameter space:
   Omega is in the phi-parameter Q. A fixed linear fractional transformation
   changes height by a fixed constant only. For a degree-six homogeneous form,
   one projective ratio determines one square class, so deletion is O(H).

5. Sign and height:
   KL pp.7--8 explicitly choose a fixed negative real neighborhood for the
   imaginary case and give exponent 2g+2=6. Directly, |F(a,b)|=O(H^6) also
   gives |Disc Q(sqrt t)|=O(H^6).
