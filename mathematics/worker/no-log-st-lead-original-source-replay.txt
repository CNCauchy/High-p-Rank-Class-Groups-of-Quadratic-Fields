Falsification checks
--------------------

1. Pair/value confusion:
   Refuted as a reading of the proof. ST first counts pairs, then (11)-(15)
   bounds multiplicity and explicitly derives C21*u^2 distinct values.

2. Unbounded representation multiplicity:
   Refuted. Stewart 1991 Corollary 1 plus ST (15) gives a fixed bound.

3. Global w substituted for final-class w:
   Refuted. Printed p.948 defines w inside the fixed A,B mod M class.

4. Positive cone silently changed:
   A real risk for a general form because p.951 permits SL2 preprocessing.
   For the C0 route, finite endpoint avoidance makes both endpoint coefficients
   nonzero before ST is applied, so the preprocessing is skipped.

5. Hidden subsequence in H:
   Not found. The proof works for every sufficiently large real u; floor(H)
   also supplies a direct all-large-real conversion.

6. Residue-class direction in the optional SL2 line:
   The printed forward action is algebraically wrong; the inverse action is
   correct. A mod-5 counterexample is recorded in the audit. This line is not
   used for C0 after endpoint avoidance.

7. Final R_k >= |T| line:
   T is a pair set, so the literal line is wrong. The immediately preceding
   distinct-value conclusion supplies the correct set V and bound.
