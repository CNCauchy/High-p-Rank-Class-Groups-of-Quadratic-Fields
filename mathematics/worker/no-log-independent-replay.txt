6. Original-source theorem anchors checked
------------------------------------------

Kulkarni-Levin arXiv:2111.15582v1:
- Theorem 1.2: fixed local neighborhoods + thin-set exclusion yield
  >> B^2/(log B)^2 distinct quadratic fields.
- Theorem 2.1: their weak Stewart-Top variation.
- Lemma 3.1: PGL2(Q) transformation encodes local conditions as a positive
  congruence class a=A mod M, b=B mod M.
- Lemma 3.2: O(B) thin rational numbers of height below B.
- proof of Theorem 1.2: distinct squarefree t give distinct Q(sqrt(t)); thin
  deletion is subtracted after choosing bounded witnesses.
- Theorem 4.1 and proof of Theorem 1.3: local ramification, class-rank bound,
  real/imaginary sign control, and |Disc| << B^(2g+2).

Stewart-Top, JAMS 8 (1995), printed pp.948-954:
- p.948: w is the maximal fixed kth-power divisor on the congruence class;
  R_k counts k-free t with F(a,b)=t*w^k.
- pp.950-951, Theorem 1: no-log lower bound R_k(x) >> x^(2/r) when the
  binary form has nonzero discriminant and maximal factor degree m<=2k+1,
  plus the k=2,m=6 boundary.
- pp.951-954, proof: the counted distinct values arise from pairs satisfying
  1<=a,b<=u and the fixed congruences, with u asymptotic to x^(1/r).
- p.954, Theorem 2: the more general but weaker /(log x)^2 theorem; this is
  not the theorem used for NO-LOG.
